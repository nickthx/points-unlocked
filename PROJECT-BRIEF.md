# Points Unlocked (working title)

## One-liner
Show people who HAVE credit card points — but no idea what to do with them — how powerful those points actually are.

## The problem
Newer points-and-miles people sit on 50K–200K points and redeem them for
$0.01/pt cash back or gift cards, or let them rot. They don't know transfer
partners, sweet spots, or that 80K points can be a business-class flight to
Europe instead of $800 of statement credit.

## v1 scope (LinkedIn demo)
1. User inputs their points balances by program (Chase UR, Amex MR, Capital One,
   Citi TY, Bilt, plus major airline/hotel programs).
2. App shows what those points can ACTUALLY do — ranked aspirational redemptions:
   - "Your 90K Amex MR → ANA business class to Tokyo (worth ~$4,500 cash)"
   - Side-by-side: cash-out value vs. transfer-partner value (the "wow" delta).
3. Redemption database is curated (sweet spots, transfer ratios, rough award
   pricing) — not live award search. Curation is the product.

## Long-term vision
Full app: user accounts, saved balances, card recommendations for the NEXT card
given goals, eligibility rules (5/24, Chase 48-month, Amex lifetime), big card +
redemption database.

## Credibility / voice
Built by an active churner: Middle East, Europe, and Africa trips in 2026 alone
on points, multiple first-class flights. The app should carry that
"here's what's possible" energy — aspirational, concrete, numbers-forward.

## Audience
- Primary: beginners with points who don't know their value.
- Secondary: LinkedIn viewers (recruiters/hiring managers) — this is a portfolio
  piece showing product thinking + full-stack execution. Production quality
  matters: real deploy (Vercel), clean UI, not a toy.
